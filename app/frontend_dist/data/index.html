Method, step by step

  1. 1
    Data audit

    Counted patients, classes and duplicates in every dataset. The Kaggle 'MIMIC-IV transcripts' file turned out to hold 7 patients, 13 admissions and no Crohn's cases (209,951 of its 232,158 rows are exact duplicates), so it was not used.

  2. 2
    Pre-processing

    Full frame at its native 320 × 320 (no cropping — lesions can sit at the edge of the circular capsule view), ImageNet normalisation.

  3. 3
    Model

    EfficientNet-B2 (7.7 M parameters) pre-trained on ImageNet, fine-tuned end-to-end for 40 epochs on an RTX 3050 GPU: AdamW, cosine schedule, label smoothing 0.1, square-root class weights for the rare classes, exponential moving average of the weights. Two training seeds; the final model averages both (decided before scoring).

  4. 4
    Augmentation

    Random rotation (any angle — the capsule view is circular), flips, ±15% zoom, brightness/contrast/saturation jitter.

  5. 5
    Validation

    Official CrohnIPI 5-fold split; one model per fold, never evaluated on its own training frames. Fixed number of epochs — no early stopping on test data. 8-view test-time augmentation (4 rotations × flip). Earlier versions (EfficientNet-B0 at 224 px, 10 and 15 epochs) are kept in the results for comparison.

  6. 6
    Severity tiers

    7 classes mapped to 4 ordinal tiers following capsule activity scores (Lewis, CECDAI) and the CrohnIPI severity order: Normal → Mild (erythema, oedema) → Moderate (aphthoid, 3–10 mm ulcer) → Severe (> 10 mm ulcer, stenosis).

  7. 7
    Explainability

    Class-activation maps from the last convolutional layer, averaged across the 10 models; model agreement and ±1 SD shown with every prediction.

  8. 8
    External tests

    Kvasir-Capsule and SEE-AI evaluated with no re-training. Heat-maps checked against expert lesion boxes (pointing game).

Limitations

• CrohnIPI has no patient IDs in its public release; frames from one patient may sit in different folds, so internal scores can be optimistic — the external tests are there to check this.

• Frame-level labels: one frame, one label. Real frames can show several lesions.

• The UC grader and the findings screen use frozen features (CPU budget) and lose accuracy on new hospitals.

• The flare model has only 61 flare events; its confidence intervals are wide.

• Datasets are European, Japanese, Turkish and US; performance on Indian patients is untested.

On hold, not dropped: onset prediction (5–6 years before diagnosis) and disease-course prediction (B1 → stricturing/penetrating, surgery) from routine hospital records. These need MIMIC-IV (credentialed access pending). The web app and validation code are built so these modules can be added without changing the interface.
