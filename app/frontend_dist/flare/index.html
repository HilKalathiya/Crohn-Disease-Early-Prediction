Flare Risk Forecaster

For a Crohn's patient currently in remission (Harvey-Bradshaw Index < 5), estimate the chance that the next visit (about two weeks later) shows a flare (HBI ≥ 5). Trained on 66 Crohn's patients followed for up to a year in the HMP2 study.
Patient timeline

Honest result. With 61 flare events in 27 patients, the random forest (AUROC 0.71) only matches a one-number baseline — the current HBI on its own (AUROC 0.71). Recent symptom trajectory carries most of the signal; more patients, or MIMIC-IV once approved, are needed before extra variables add value.
