Module 1c · whole capsule study

Whole-Video Scan

Scan any capsule video below. Further down, each Kvasir-Capsule video is one complete capsule study of about 50,000 frames (Norway, Olympus capsule, never used for training). The scan scored every 10th frame with one fold model, then re-scored the 150 most suspicious frames with all 5 models (the EfficientNet-B0 v2 models, sized for a laptop CPU); clicking a frame runs the current 10-model ensemble on it live. Red ticks mark frames that experts labelled as ulcer, erosion or erythema.
Scan your own capsule video

Upload a video, or copy it into the videos folder and press Refresh (better for files of several GB). One network scores about 2,000 frames spread over the video; the most suspicious frames are then re-scored by all networks of the ensemble. Everything runs on this computer.
